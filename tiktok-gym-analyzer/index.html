    Posting schedule

    How often

    Post 1–2 videos per day, every day, for 30 days. Consistency beats perfection — the algorithm rewards volume + watch time while you're small. Quality matters, but a "perfect" video once a week will not grow you.

    Best times (Serbia time)

    Your followers are mostly students + gym-goers. Post ~30 min before they're free:

    • Mon–Fri7:00–8:00 (morning scroll) · 14:00–16:00 (after school) · 19:00–22:00 (prime, best)
    • Sat–Sun11:00–13:00 · 19:00–22:00

    After 1–2 weeks, check your own Analytics → Followers → most active times and adjust to YOUR audience.

    Stories — what & how often

    Post 2–4 stories/day. Stories build loyalty with people who already follow you (they don't grow reach, but they turn viewers into fans):

    • •Behind the scenes: walking to the gym, pre-workout, the boring real grind.
    • •Polls/questions: "Push or pull today?", "Rate my form" — replies boost your relationship score.
    • •Daily check-in: meal, water, mood — show the discipline, not just highlights.
    • •Re-share your post to story so more followers catch it.
