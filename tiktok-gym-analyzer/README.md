# Gym TikTok Coach 💪

Your private, personal TikTok growth assistant — built for a gym / aesthetics / David-Laid-path creator from Pirot, Serbia.

It is **one single file** (`index.html`). No login, no internet needed, no one else can see your data. Everything you type is saved **only on your phone**.

## How to open it on your phone

**Easiest way (recommended):**
1. Email `index.html` to yourself, or send it to yourself on Telegram/WhatsApp.
2. Open it on your phone and tap "Open in browser".
3. In the browser menu tap **"Add to Home Screen"** — now it looks and opens like a real app.

**From your computer:** just double-click `index.html` and it opens in any browser.

## What it does

| Tab | What it gives you |
|-----|-------------------|
| 🏠 **Home** | Your stats + this week's focus |
| 🔬 **Analyze** | Type a video's views/likes/comments/shares → it tells you **why** it did well or poorly and **exactly what to fix** |
| 📁 **Videos** | History of every video you analyzed, graded (Winner / Hidden gem / Needs work…) |
| 💡 **Ideas** | Endless gym-niche video ideas + scroll-stopping hooks |
| #️⃣ **Tags** | Smart hashtag sets (broad + niche + local Serbia) — tap to copy |
| 📅 **Plan** | How often to post, best times for Serbia, story plan, weekly template |
| 🔥 **Trends** | Evergreen gym trend formats + how to find what's hot this week |
| 👤 **Profile** | Bio templates + a checklist to make your profile convert visitors into followers |
| 🚀 **30-Day** | A day-by-day glow-up plan you check off |

## How the analysis works (so you trust it)

For each video it calculates your **engagement rate** = (likes + comments + shares) ÷ views, plus like/comment/share/save rates, and compares the views to **your own account average**. From that it figures out the real problem:

- **High engagement but low views** → content is good, the *hook* or *watch time* is killing reach → re-post with a stronger first second.
- **High views but low engagement** → looked good, no payoff → add an ending / a reason to react.
- **Low both** → almost always the first 2 seconds + video length.
- **High both** → that's your winning formula → make a Part 2.

Shares and saves are weighted heavily because those are the strongest "push this video" signals on TikTok.

## Privacy

100% offline. Your data lives in your browser's local storage on one device. Use **Export** (Profile tab) to back it up or move to a new phone.
